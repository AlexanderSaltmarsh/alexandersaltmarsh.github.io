Combustion Simulation

I got familiar with STANJAN and Cantera combustion simulation platforms for a course on aircraft & rocket propulsion. The most interesting part was playing with reaction conditions and seeing what different chemical species were produced. Using these software tools reminded me how much I enjoy studying and applying chemistry. Also, it was quite fun using a local emulation of MS DOS to properly execute the STANJAN simulations. Digging around old OS environments makes me appreciate how streamlined everything is now.

Feb 2026

Game Theory & Mixed Equilibria

I got really interested in Game Theory after taking a class on the subject. I've been looking into mixed strategies and how they apply beyond just financial markets. The idea of finding "stable points" seems to be a recurring theme. Whether that's in a combustion engine, an operating system, game drivers, anything. I feel like Game Theory is uniquely applicable to all those fields. You could make weighting matrices for pretty much anything and have an analysis that may produce meaningful insight. Really glad I could dive into the subject.
