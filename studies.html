Combustion Simulation

I had the chance to try out combustion simulation software in an aircraft/rocket propulsion class. I thought this was really interesting. More specifically, I used STANJAN and Cantera to run the sims. I think the most interesting part was playing with the reactions and seeing what conditions produced specific chemical species. I haven't really had a chance to do much applied chemistry recently, and using these software tools reminded me how much fun that was. Also, figuring out how to run STANJAN by running a local emulation of MS DOS was fun. I like digging around old OS environments.

Feb 2026

Game Theory & Mixed Equilibria

I took a class on Game Theory and it really sparked my interest in the subject. I've been looking more into mixed strategies and how they apply beyond just financial markets. The idea of finding "stable points" seems to be a recurring theme. Whether that's in a combustion engine, an operating system, game drivers, anything. I feel like Game Theory is uniquely applicable to all those fields and not in a BS generalized way. You could make weighting matrices for pretty much anything and have a game theoretical analysis produce some meaningful insight. Really glad I could take this class and get an introduction on the subject.
