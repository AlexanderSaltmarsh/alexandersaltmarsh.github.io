Rocket Test Stand

Test Stand for the "Phoenix" Liquid Bipropellant Rocket on UCSD's Rocketry Team. I had the chance to learn waterjetting and basic MIG welding for this project, really enjoyable experience. The video at the end shows the stand surviving a particularly fiery test.

"Hermes" Solid-Fuel Rocket

Designed and manufactured the Thrust-Chamber-Assembly for the "Hermes" solid-fuel rocket. Also aided in the design and manufacturing of the internal structure, segment spacers, avionics housing, and recovery system.

Human Powered Submarine

I worked to help make the hull for the submarine on UCSD's Human Powered Sub team. Though I didn't end up staying with the team too long, I still really value the chance to help contribute and work on the sub.
