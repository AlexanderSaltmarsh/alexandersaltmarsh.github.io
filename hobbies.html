Recently Playing

Games That Inspire Me

Civilization IV

First game I ever played. I have many fond memories playing it with my Dad, I truly can't exagerrate the role that Civ IV played in inspiring my passion for history at a young age. This game formed some of my earliest and most fundamental interests; metallurgy, negotiation, exploration, flight. The music within this game is phenomenal, it has an incredible soundtrack packed with great classical and modern (particularly modern minimalist) pieces. It presented an incredible sandbox for me to mess around and learn from. Also, it's pretty damn fun. Civ IV sticks with me, I'm very glad to have had the chance to play it.

Witcher 3

I really enjoy the Witcher. Really cool world and monsters. Riding around and exploring the wilderness was extremely fun. Also really enjoyed exploring the different locations, Skellige in particular. Sailing around the coast and doing treasure hunts was pretty great too.

Rome Total War

The scale in this game rocked my brain. Conquering the classical world, playing out the rise of Rome, figuring out the strengths and weaknesses of different military units. Learning about the different cultures and the history behind each faction. Barbarian Invasion Expansion was awesome as well. Greeks & Brutii always on top.
